How do I ask about a figure?

Send us a message on Instagram and mention the figure or collection you’re interested in. We’ll get back to you with current availability and details.

Where can I find prices and shipping information?

Prices, payment options, and delivery details can vary. Please confirm the latest information directly with PiToys before placing an order.

Can I ask about size, materials, or age suitability?

Product specifications and age recommendations are not listed in this catalog. Contact us for verified details about a specific figure before ordering.
